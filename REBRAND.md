## What's NOT IP
- The name "GOLDEN SHOWER" — not trademarked by Bond franchise
- Arena FPS gameplay — generic game mechanic
- P2P WebRTC multiplayer — our own KQTT protocol
- 4-player splitscreen layout — generic
- Weapon archetypes (pistol, rifle, shotgun, sniper, rocket, one-shot-kill)
- Map layouts (the geometry is original, only names are IP)
